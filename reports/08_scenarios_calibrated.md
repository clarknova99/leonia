## Reading the spillover flag

A link is flagged as **spillover** when its baseline flow was <200 veh/h (typically residential) but the scenario adds >50 veh/h. A high spillover count means the scenario is pushing cut-through traffic onto smaller streets instead of eliminating it. Compared to v1, the v2 model uses real OD volumes for the GWB approach, so spillover numbers should reflect the actual diversion that the real morning commuter flow would induce.
